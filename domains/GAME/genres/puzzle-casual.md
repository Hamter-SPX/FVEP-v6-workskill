# Puzzle / casual

Short sessions, clear goals, strong juice, level packs, low learning friction.

## Content pillars

- Level goal clarity in under 5 seconds
- Fail/retry loop feel
- Juice (VFX/SFX/haptics) without noise
- Meta progression / soft currency (if any)
- Level pipeline tooling

## UI/UX hotspots

- First-run teaching without walls of text
- Win/lose modals and next-level CTA
- Settings and accessibility (colorblind, reduce motion)
