# Open-world action

Shape similar to large action sandboxes (e.g. GTA-class): traversable city, vehicles, missions, law/wanted pressure, dense interactive props.

## Content pillars

- World streaming and districts
- Player traversal + vehicle feel
- Mission / activity graph
- NPC / traffic / emergency services AI
- Economy, shops, collectibles
- Wanted / heat systems
- Audio beds that change with area and threat

## UI/UX hotspots

- HUD in motion (speed, weapons, minimap)
- Pause map and mission brief
- Phone/radial menus without breaking immersion
- Wanted and damage feedback readable at speed

## Asset / plugin pressure

- Huge environment + vehicle + ped catalogs
- LOD / impostors / streaming budgets
- Middleware: physics, audio, animation; custom mission tools
