# UGC avatar platform graphics

Roblox-class look: modular bodies, catalog clothing/accessories, many creators, inconsistent quality unless gated.

## Pipeline pressure

- Avatar assembly from parts (head, torso, layered clothing)
- Catalog thumbnails and rarity/presentation UI
- Performance with many unique player meshes on screen
- Moderation of user-uploaded decals/meshes

## Direction caution

Platform chrome (menus, shop) is often shared; experience art must still read clearly on diverse avatars.
