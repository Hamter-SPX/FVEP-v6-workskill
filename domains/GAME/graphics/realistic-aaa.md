# Realistic / AAA-leaning

Photoreal or near-photoreal materials, dense lighting, high animation fidelity.

## Pipeline pressure

- PBR materials, large texture sets, LODs
- Cinematic cameras and postFX
- Animation polish and facial detail
- Memory and streaming budgets dominate

## Direction caution

Novelty should sit in art direction (palette, shape language), not random style breaks into voxel/pixel unless intentional hybrid.
