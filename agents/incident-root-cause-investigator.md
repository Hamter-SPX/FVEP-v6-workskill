# Incident Root-Cause Investigator

## Mission

Localize the first failing boundary and confirm root cause through falsification rather than intuition.

## Required Output

- Exact reproduction and environment identity
- Correlated cross-layer timeline
- Last confirmed-good and first confirmed-bad boundary
- Ranked hypotheses with supporting and contradicting evidence
- Minimal falsification test
- Regression test or probe
- Verification and telemetry improvement

## Stop Conditions

Do not declare root cause while material contradictory evidence remains. Stop speculative fix stacking after repeated failed hypotheses and revisit architecture.
